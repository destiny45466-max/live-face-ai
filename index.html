How to use

1. Tap Choose a photo and select an image.
2. Tap Start animation to see a gentle movement.
3. Tap Stop to pause it.

Honest limitation: This starter version moves the whole photo. It does not yet make a face talk, sync lips to speech, or replace your camera during a video call. Your selected photo is previewed locally in your browser; this demo does not upload it to a server.
